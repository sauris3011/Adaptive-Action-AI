# Challenge 1: Adaptive Knowledge-to-Action Copilot

## Title

Adaptive Knowledge-to-Action Copilot for Complex Work

## Problem Statement

Organizations across industries struggle to convert fragmented knowledge into timely, reliable action.

Policies, manuals, emails, tickets, operational data and expert know-how are often distributed across disconnected systems, while employees and customers expect fast, context-aware support.

Traditional search and rule-based automation return information but rarely understand intent, reconcile conflicting sources, explain recommendations or complete multi-step work.

The challenge is to design an AI solution that can understand a user goal, retrieve and reason over enterprise knowledge, collaborate with humans and systems, and safely translate insights into actions.

The solution should remain broad enough to adapt to domains such as healthcare, banking, manufacturing, retail, public services, telecom, education or energy.

## Data Requirements (Guidance)

Enterprise documents, FAQs, policies, product or service catalogues, process maps, historical tickets, chat or email interactions, transactional records, workflow events and user feedback.

Teams may use public, synthetic or organizer-provided data.

Data may be structured, unstructured, multimodal or multilingual.

Solutions should consider access control, data lineage, privacy, freshness, hallucination management and evaluation of answer quality.

(Feel free to generate synthetic data as well from the provided access.)

## Expected Output / Demonstration of Solution

A working prototype of an adaptive copilot that supports question answering, decision support and at least one end-to-end action workflow.

The demonstration may include retrieval-augmented generation, knowledge graphs, multimodal understanding, agentic orchestration, tool use, human approval, confidence scoring and explainable recommendations.

Show how the solution can be configured for a preferred industry, measured for accuracy and usefulness, monitored for safety, and scaled through modular APIs or services.

Include measurable KPIs such as task completion time, resolution quality, user effort, automation rate or compliance adherence.

Be creative and go beyond the stated needs while keeping the solution responsible, measurable and adaptable.

## Business Goal

Build an adaptive AI copilot that turns fragmented enterprise knowledge into fast, reliable and actionable outcomes. The solution should understand user intent, retrieve and reconcile relevant information, provide explainable recommendations, and complete selected business workflows with appropriate human oversight. It should reduce task completion time and user effort, improve resolution quality and compliance, and increase safe automation. The copilot must be reusable across industries, support multiple data types and systems, and remain measurable, secure, scalable and adaptable as business processes, policies and knowledge change.
