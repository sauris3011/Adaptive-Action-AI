## REG-E-1 — Scope

This summary applies to electronic fund transfers from consumer asset accounts, including debit
card transactions. It does not extend to credit card transactions, which are governed by
Regulation Z and by the applicable card network rules.

## REG-E-2 — Notice window

A consumer must report an unauthorised electronic fund transfer within 60 calendar days of the
statement on which the transfer first appears. An institution may, at its discretion, extend this
window; it may not shorten it.

## REG-E-3 — Provisional credit ceiling

Where an investigation is not complete, the institution must issue provisional credit for the
disputed amount no later than 10 business days after receiving notice of the error. For accounts
opened within the preceding 30 days the permitted period extends to 20 business days.

The 10 business day figure is a regulatory **maximum**, not a target. Nothing in this section
prevents an institution from crediting earlier, and a contractual commitment to credit earlier is
enforceable against the institution.

## REG-E-4 — Investigation period

The institution must complete its investigation within 45 calendar days of notice, extended to 90
calendar days for point-of-sale, foreign-initiated, or new-account transactions.

## REG-E-5 — Notice of outcome

The consumer must be notified of the outcome within three business days of completing the
investigation. Where credit already issued is reversed, the notice must state the reversal date and
the reason.
