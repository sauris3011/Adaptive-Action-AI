## GRS-1.1 — Instrument definition

A goodwill refund is a courtesy credit funded from the bank's own customer-resolution budget. It is
not a chargeback, it does not recover funds from the merchant, and it does not open a scheme dispute.

## GRS-2.3 — Auto-approval threshold

Goodwill refunds of less than 50 USD are auto-approved at agent level with no merchant contact
required and no team lead sign-off.

## GRS-2.7 — Above-threshold handling

Goodwill refunds of 50 USD or more require Team Lead approval and a recorded justification.

## GRS-4.1 — Interaction with chargebacks

Issuing a goodwill refund does not prevent a chargeback from being filed subsequently for the same
transaction, but the recoverable amount is reduced by the goodwill credit already issued. Agents must
record both instruments against the same case.

## GRS-5.2 — Frequency limit

No more than three goodwill refunds may be issued to a single customer in any rolling 12-month
period without Team Lead approval.
