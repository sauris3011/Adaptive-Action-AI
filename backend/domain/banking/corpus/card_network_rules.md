## CNR-4.2 — Chargeback filing windows

A chargeback must be submitted within 120 calendar days of the transaction processing date, or of
the date the goods or services were expected to be received, whichever is later. The outer limit is
540 calendar days from the processing date.

## CNR-6.1 — Reason code selection

The issuer must select the reason code that matches the cardholder's account of events. Filing under
a fraud reason code where the cardholder acknowledges authorising the transaction is a misuse of the
scheme and may be reversed on pre-arbitration.

## CNR-11.4 — Goods or services not received

Before filing a "goods or services not received" chargeback, the issuer must confirm that the
cardholder attempted to resolve the matter with the merchant, and must record the date and outcome
of that contact. A chargeback filed without evidence of merchant contact is subject to representment.

This requirement attaches to the chargeback instrument. It does not govern any goodwill or courtesy
credit the issuer chooses to fund from its own accounts.

## CNR-12.9 — Fraud reporting obligations

Transactions confirmed as fraudulent must be reported to the scheme's fraud reporting service within
30 calendar days of confirmation, irrespective of whether a chargeback is filed.
