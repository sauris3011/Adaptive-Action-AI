## PCT-1.1 — Eligibility

These terms apply to holders of the Signature Premium Card. Holders of Standard or Classic cards are
governed by the Standard Cardholder Agreement.

## PCT-3.2 — Zero liability

The cardholder bears no liability for unauthorised transactions reported within the notice window,
provided the cardholder has not disclosed their credentials to a third party.

## PCT-7.1 — Accelerated provisional credit

For Signature cardholders, the bank issues provisional credit for a disputed transaction within
2 business days of the dispute being raised, where the investigation remains open.

This is a contractual commitment to the cardholder and is more favourable than the standard bank
timeline.

## PCT-9.4 — Concierge escalation

Signature cardholders may request escalation to the concierge desk at any point during a dispute.
Escalation does not alter the provisional credit timeline stated in PCT-7.1.
