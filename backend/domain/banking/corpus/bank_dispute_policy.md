## BDP-1.1 — Purpose

This policy governs the intake, investigation, and resolution of cardholder disputes across all card
products issued by the bank.

## BDP-2.1 — Unauthorised transaction intake

Where a cardholder reports a transaction they did not authorise, the agent opens a dispute case,
records the cardholder's statement, and places the disputed amount under investigation. The case is
assigned to the Disputes queue for standard handling.

This clause governs intake only. It does not override any routing requirement established by the
Fraud Operations policy.

## BDP-3.4 — Agent authority limits

An agent may approve provisional credit up to 500 USD. Amounts above 500 USD require approval by a
Disputes Team Lead. No provisional credit of any amount is issued without a recorded approver.

## BDP-4.2 — Provisional credit timing

Provisional credit for a disputed transaction is issued within 10 business days of case creation
where the investigation remains open.

Where the cardholder's product terms commit the bank to a shorter period, the shorter period applies.

## BDP-5.3 — Evidence requirements

The case file must record the cardholder's statement, the transaction record, any merchant contact
attempted, and the clause under which the outcome was decided.

## BDP-7.1 — Deadline calculation

Business days exclude weekends and published bank holidays. The clock starts on the first business
day after the cardholder's notice is received.
